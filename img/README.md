## Application icons

`icon.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` are **original** drawings: two chequered flags crossed in an X (like the Race tab icon), whose dark squares take the colors of the LEDs, from green to red,
with an orange outline. The icons have a **transparent background** (mid-gray pole and orange outline to stay readable on both light and dark backgrounds), except `apple-touch-icon.png`, deliberately opaque on a dark background: iOS fills transparency with black. They do not reuse any existing logo and are not subject to a trademark. They are generated by `tools/make-icons.py` (dependency: `cairosvg`):
change the colors or the drawing in that script and rerun it, rather than editing the files by hand.
